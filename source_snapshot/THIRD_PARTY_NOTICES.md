## Nested Notice Follow-Up

- NumPy: collect the OpenBLAS, LAPACK, GCC runtime, and libquadmath notices from
  the locked wheel before release.
- Matplotlib: collect all bundled font, palette, Qhull, JavaScript, and related
  `LICENSE_*` texts from the locked wheel.
- pandas, Pillow, PyArrow, and fontTools: reconcile native or embedded component
  notices against the eventual P0-B2B onedir inventory.
- Streamlit: obtain an authoritative Apache-2.0 license/notice source because the
  resolved wheel does not contain a license file.
- Biopython and python-dateutil: complete license-term review before release.

Primer3 and pydna are intentionally absent because they are not distributed in
the V1 base runtime.
